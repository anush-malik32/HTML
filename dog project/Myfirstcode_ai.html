Adopt a Dog For Free

There are over 1 million dogs that do not get good homes and are left homeless without anyone to take care of them. Well I am doing my best to make sure these dogs get loving and caring homes by taking over 1 hundred dogs and having a state-wide adoption event in Chicago. Please come visit and give these dogs a good and loving home for the rest of their life.

Benefits of Adopting A Dog

  • You have a social companion that is always your friend no matter what.
  • You make everyone in your household smiling until it hurts and you will be smiling yourself because having a dog can do that to you.
  • Finally, having a dog can make you more athletic because you have to keep up with that ball of energy all the time.
